## Synthetic evidence binding

The new fixture-binding prototype verifies source/artifact hashes and the
record's exact track/clock/segment against a bounded synthetic JSON artifact.
It computes sounding-time union, restticks, onset count and distinct sounding
pitches, including held notes beginning before the segment and excluding onsets
at its end. Overlap is not double-counted. Changing exact bytes or clock/scope
fails validation. No real MIDI or listener comment is ingested.

Three fictional evidence-layer examples bind to the same constructed artifact.
Within384ticks they contain276sounding and108restticks,3onsets and3sounding
pitches. These are arithmetic observations, not confirmation of free-text
wording. Every binding explicitly reports wording unverified, actor/origin
unverified, consent unverified and training ineligible. Digest agreement alone
cannot prove authorship, synthetic origin, truth or authority. Even an approved
review string does not grant training. Actual human reports and semantic labels
remain zero.
