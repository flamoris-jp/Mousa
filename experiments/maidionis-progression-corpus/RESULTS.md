# Results and failed attempts

## Intake and extraction

The collection review retained 22 historical-edition-reviewed sets / 198 score
entries. 191 exact encoded files were available. The final conservative parser
accepted 78 scores, covering 3,854 written bars; 1,718 bars received a non-null
weak chord label. Not every accepted bar contributes a training window.

Rejected source/format cases were preserved: seven absent exact files, 43
MuseScore 2 encodings, 17 non-matching CC0 metadata markers, 36 changing/missing
key signatures, nine changing/missing meters, one unsupported key signature and
seven unsupported Volta-span scores. These are pilot admissibility boundaries,
not musical defects.

### Parser corrections, not threshold tuning

1. The first parser mistakenly treated slur/tie Spanner reference locations as
   voice-cursor movements. It left only two training and two test eight-context
   windows. No fit was attempted.
2. Context-sensitive location handling was corrected and regression-tested.
   The old artifacts and source hashes were retained. This produced 115 / 14
   eight-context windows. It still failed the frozen minimum test-size gate.
3. Independent review identified MuseScore's `<Spanner type="Volta">` form.
   The final parser conservatively excludes whole scores containing such spans.
   With all original split assignments and numerical thresholds unchanged, the
   eight-context result was 111 / 14. This experiment remains **not fitted**.

Raw duration, label-confidence and family-split criteria were not relaxed in
response to prediction performance. No prediction performance existed during
these corrections.

## Separate local-transition task

Quantity-only feasibility considered contexts of one and two prior bars. Two
was chosen to retain more local information while passing the same minimum
size gate. The separate local2 preparation contains:

- **Train: 620 windows (285 distinct three-chord patterns), 45 scores, 12 set-level families.**
- **Test: 102 windows, 12 scores, five disjoint set-level families.**

Two training families supply 349 / 620 windows. Thus row-weighted learning and
metrics are not repertoire-balanced; family-macro evaluation is also needed.
The entire corpus is a narrow historical art-song subset, not a broad inventory
of pop, jazz, modal, chromatic or contemporary chord progressions.

The local2 protocol was fixed before its own preparation and before any fit,
not before the earlier extraction/quantity inspection. It is not a preregistered
blind study of untouched scores. It was selected on sample counts, not test
prediction performance. Historical exposure by earlier research was not
established; the held-out families are unseen by this newly initialized model.

## Interpretation boundaries

The labels summarize pitch-duration mass in an entire written bar and can
misrepresent multiple chords, non-chord tones, mode, local key and phrase
function. Agreement with these labels measures imitation of a weak heuristic,
not agreement with a human harmonic analyst. The score provides timing alignment,
but this model does not jointly learn melody, articulation, rhythm or phrasing.

The auditions fix C major, 4/4, 100 BPM and eight bars. Only the chord progression
is generated by the local model. The exact same self-authored C-major arpeggio
melody is used in every condition; clashes may reflect this deliberate control,
not an independently learned melody. No human preference result is claimed.

Long-window copy checking cannot be inferred from local2 windows alone.
Local trigram matches are only a limited repetition diagnostic and cannot
establish novelty, copyright clearance, or absence of memorization.

## One fixed local2 fit

A fresh seed-314 model completed exactly 60 × 32 sampled observations in
3.873488 CPU seconds. There were 1,920 draws, 586 distinct rows (276 distinct three-chord patterns from 44 scores) and 11 sampled
training families; the available 12th family was not drawn in this run. Saved
weights differ from initialization, and reloaded logits matched exactly.
No second fit, seed selection or test-directed tuning was performed.

| Model | Test accuracy (102 rows) | NLL, lower is better | Family-macro accuracy | Family-macro NLL |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| exposure_matched_trigram | 18.63% | 2.5692 | 20.45% | 2.5193 |
| exposure_matched_unigram | 10.78% | 2.6096 | 8.35% | 2.6255 |
| full_train_trigram | 19.61% | 2.4948 | 21.10% | 2.4570 |
| full_train_unigram | 10.78% | 2.5984 | 8.35% | 2.6132 |
| initial_ai | 0.00% | 2.8974 | 0.00% | 2.9673 |
| trained_ai | 19.61% | 2.7760 | 19.46% | 2.7857 |

The fitted model improved over its untrained initialization, but **did not
establish an advantage over simple count models**. Its 20/102 correct predictions
equal the full-data trigram baseline; its NLL is worse than all fitted count
baselines and even the uniform 14-class NLL (log(14) ≈ 2.639). Exposure-matched
trigram accuracy is 19/102, only one answer less, while its NLL is better.
This is not evidence of superior harmony or good calibration. Overlapping
windows and only five test families preclude treating 102 as independent songs.

## Audition verification

Four 8-bar clips were rendered: fixed I–V–vi–IV × 2, and unselected seeds
17 / 29 / 43. All are 19.2 seconds of MIDI plus two seconds of audio tail,
21.2 seconds each. Cantabile import/validate/export preserved MIDI message
tracks; this is not a byte-identity claim. All four received a common gain of
12, with no PCM clipping (peak amplitudes 10,428–11,400 / 32,767). No human
listening or aesthetic preference was recorded.

An additional independent full-source screen compared each generated eight-label
sequence with 144 contiguous training-side eight-bar occurrences (108 unique)
from 64 extracted scores. All four had zero exact matches in this narrow screen.
This neither rules out shorter reuse nor proves originality or copyright safety.
The source-conditioned trigram overlap ranges from 0/6 to 6/6 across AI clips;
common local transitions are expected, not proof of theft or creativity.

Two early render invocations stopped before inference because a conservative
output guard mistook runtime placeholder .git directories for actual worktrees.
The guard was corrected to ask Git for a real worktree, regression-tested on
both a real repository and a placeholder. It continues to reject actual Git
worktrees. No generation seed was rerolled in these setup failures.

## Verification summary

The final Python suite ran 124 tests with one documented skip: the optional
external stem-exporter integration was not configured. Both native context
profiles passed 23 no-fit contract tests each; four cross-profile/boundary tests
also passed. Independent audit verified source-window alignment, all evaluation
aggregates, checkpoint hashes, actual sampling order, 53 parameters / 106 Adam
moment tensors at step 60, and the changed initial embedding. No independent
audit performed an extra fit or model-forward generation.
