## Semantic proposal, not synthetic ground truth

The owner's suggestion to supply semantics is recorded as a prospective design
in docs/SEMANTIC_ANNOTATION_RESEARCH.md. It separates observable symbolic/source
facts, literal human impressions/intent, and researcher/model hypotheses. A
small ending interval is not automatically a cadence label, and a hypothetical
emotion is not a listener's answer. No semantic labels or human feedback were
created, fitted or treated as approved data. Optional comments need artifact/
interval scope, original wording and permitted use; identifying feedback stays
private. The quantity experiments and fixed source gates are unchanged.
